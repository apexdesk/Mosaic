Mosaic

Every reference, on one board.

Drop in images, videos, PDFs, 3D models and sound, write notes, sketch over them and keep it all in a single file. It stays fast with hundreds of photos on the canvas.

Performance

Big boards, no waiting.

Mosaic only loads what you can see, at the size you see it. A board of hundreds of full-resolution photos opens like a small one.

0.8s

199 photos on screen (289 MB of JPEGs), from launch.

0.44s

To reopen a 282 MB board with everything painted.

0%

CPU while idle. Nothing is redrawn until something changes.

Features

Collect, arrange, compare.

Everything happens on one infinite canvas. Zoom from the whole board down to a single brush stroke.

Collect

Drop anything in

  • Drag files or whole folders onto the window, or images straight from your browser.
  • Paste with Ctrl+V: an image, file paths or a web address.
  • Videos play in a loop, PDFs turn pages, 3D models rotate, audio shows its waveform.
  • Drag any item back out into Krita, Photoshop, Blender or your file manager and it arrives as the original file.
Arrange

Put order in the chaos

  • Pack a messy pile into a tidy grid with one key, or match heights for a clean row.
  • Group references in frames with a title; frames move what they hold.
  • Add notes with fonts, colours and alignment, and sketch freehand on top.
  • Crop without losing anything, flip, rotate, view in greys to judge values.
Work

Keep it next to your canvas

  • Make the whole window see-through and keep it on top to trace over another program.
  • Show a grid or switch to the blueprint theme.
  • Turn 3D models in place, in perspective or orthographic view.
  • The interface speaks ten languages and follows your system.
Why Mosaic

Light, local and yours.

Native and GPU-accelerated

Written in Rust. Images go to the graphics card at the detail you need, so zooming and panning stay smooth.

One file per board

A .refs board holds the original files, untouched and without duplicates. Move it, back it up or send it: nothing gets lost.

No account, no tracking

Everything stays on your computer. It only goes online to fetch an image you drag in from a browser.

No AI

Nothing is generated and nothing you collect is sent to any model.

Easy on memory

Imported files are mapped from disk instead of copied into RAM, and video memory stays within a fixed budget.

Everywhere you draw

Windows, macOS and Linux (Wayland and X11), on Intel, AMD and ARM.
